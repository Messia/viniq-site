Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Oleh Tatarchuk (“we”) handles personal data in the VINIQ app. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK/Ukraine users)

Performance of our contract with you (providing the App and your purchases), our legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention, legal obligations, and your consent where we ask for it.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Settings → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and the data linked to it (reports, saved vehicles, PDFs, credits, promo redemptions) and revokes the Sign in with Apple authorization. Backups and logs are purged on their normal cycle. Cached provider data keyed only by VIN contains no personal data and may be retained for up to 30 days. Apple keeps its own records of your purchases.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Use Settings → Delete Account or email tatarchuk.design@gmail.com.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit; sensitive tokens are encrypted at rest; each user can only access their own rows through database access rules; private PDFs are served by short-lived signed links. No system is perfectly secure.

7. Children

VINIQ is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above; material changes will also be shown in the App.
