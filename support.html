Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your reports, saved vehicles, generated PDFs, credits and your sign-in link, and revokes the Sign in with Apple authorization. Purchases made with your Apple ID are not refunded by deleting the account.

I bought credits but my balance did not update.

Keep the app open on a stable connection for a minute; unfinished purchases are retried automatically the next time the app starts. If the balance is still wrong, email us with the date of purchase and the Apple receipt from your email.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. When Apple approves a refund, the matching credits are removed from your balance automatically.

The equipment list looks wrong for my car.

Equipment data comes from third-party sources and can be incomplete or inaccurate, especially for older, modified, damaged or market-specific vehicles. Always verify the car in person. Send us the VIN and what differs and we will investigate.

Does a credit expire?

No. Credits stay on your account until used or until you delete the account. They have no cash value.
